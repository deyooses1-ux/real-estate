Frequently Asked Questions

How are down payments and remaining balances calculated on EstateSphere?

Every property displays its total price, required down payment percentage, exact down payment amount, and remaining balance (Total Price minus Down Payment). You can customize the down payment percentage, interest rate, and loan tenure inside our interactive Down Payment & Mortgage Calculator on every property page and on the Financing page.

How does multi-currency pricing work?

You can switch between USD, EUR, GBP, INR, AED, SGD, CAD, AUD, and JPY from the top navigation bar. EstateSphere fetches live reference exchange rates when available and falls back cleanly to demo reference rates. Whenever a converted display price is shown, the property's original asking price and currency are clearly displayed alongside it.

Are mortgage rates and investment yields guaranteed?

No. All mortgage payments, rental yields, and appreciation projections are illustrative estimates only. Actual lending terms depend on your independent lender and country regulations, and investment returns are never guaranteed.

How are my favourites, demo listings, and demo account stored?

EstateSphere operates completely client-side using HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript. Your favourites, comparison selections, recently viewed properties, saved searches, enquiry logs, submitted listings (tagged as Awaiting Verification), and demonstration account are stored locally in your browser's localStorage.

Privacy Policy (Demo Notice)

This demonstration website does not transmit personal data, passwords, or property submissions to any external backend server. All form entries and demo account profiles remain strictly inside your local browser storage and can be cleared anytime through your browser settings.
