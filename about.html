Our Story

Redefining How the World Discovers Residential Architecture

Founded by architects and international real estate advisors, EstateSphere was built to solve a persistent problem in cross-border property acquisition: cluttered portals filled with unverified listings, opaque down payment expectations, and fragmented local guidance.

Today, EstateSphere operates across twelve major residential markets — pairing editorial architectural presentation with instant multi-currency clarity, upfront down payment schedules, and bespoke advisory services from acquisition to ongoing management.
